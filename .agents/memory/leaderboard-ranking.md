Sort leaderboard entries by total raw points earned, descending. Show percentage as a secondary display metric only; it must not place a lower-point student above a higher-point student.

**Why:** On 2026-10-12, the user clarified that a student with 50 points must rank above a student with 30 points even if the latter has a higher percentage.

**How to apply:** Keep ranking and displayed percentage separate when changing leaderboard aggregation or presentation.
